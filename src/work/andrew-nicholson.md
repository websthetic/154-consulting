body:
  - He is an athlete, an operator behind a group of ventures, and the founder of a youth foundation, and the brief was to carry all three without any of them burying the others. Each of those audiences turns up wanting something different, and the usual answer is three separate sites.
  - The identity came first. Two directions went up. One built an A out of arrows with his jersey number carved from the negative space. The other fused the A and the N into a single monogram. He took the monogram. Orange against charcoal, drawn to hold at the size of a profile picture as well as on a wall.
  - Then the site. One place, three ways in, and the reader picks. Standing gets established by what he has actually done rather than by ranking anything, and a draggable timeline runs the foundation forward from the draft.
  - "The temptation was three sites, or three tabs, which is the same thing with extra steps. Instead the page moves through him in the order a stranger would ask: the career that makes him worth listening to, the business that shows what he does now, then the foundation, which is the part he wants you to leave thinking about."
