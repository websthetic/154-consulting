  - The identity had to stretch across four sub-brands without snapping. The WM monogram holds the parent. Underneath it, Cool Made, Fresh Made, Fuel Made and Kids Made each get their own visual reading, with a bottle, a leaf, a gas pump and a banana worked into the marks.
  - Caramel, Pistachio Sage and Vanilla underneath. Kenyan Coffee over Newsreader and Poppins. The real test of a system like this is whether a new sub-brand can join later without anybody redesigning anything.
  - The risk in a family like this is that the children start competing with the parent. Each sub-brand gets its own colour and one small signal, and nothing else. The block, the weight and the cut stay fixed, so the four read as one company making four things rather than four companies sharing a shelf.
