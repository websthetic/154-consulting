    - <b>A board, not a book.</b> Not every business needs a guidelines document. The shop needed a mark that reads on a wall, on a shirt, on a card and on the side of a van, so that is what got built.
    - <b>Set out before drawn.</b> Two circles on one vertical, tangent to each other. That is the entire construction, and the reason the mark never looks drawn by hand.
    - <b>Then traced.</b> Two wheels stack into an S, and the overlap leaves the figure eight that gives the shop its shape on a van door.
    - <b>Filled, and named.</b> Southwest Wheels and Custom sets beside it, with the icon holding alone anywhere the name is already on the wall.
    - <b>Then the lockups.</b> The icon alone for a centre cap and a shirt, horizontal for a van door, stacked for a card. Three arrangements out of one drawing, which is all a shop this size needs.
    - <b>Then the system.</b> Phantom Black and Burnt Orange. Orange never carries body text on white, because at 2.9:1 it cannot. It is a ground and a mark, and on black it does the rest of the work.
