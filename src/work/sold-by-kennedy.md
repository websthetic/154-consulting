body:
  - "Her old site looked like every other agent's, so we rebuilt it as a personal brand platform. The work started from a different question: what would make somebody choose her before they have ever met her? That turned into a site organised around her judgement rather than her inventory."
  - Listings feed in live from the board. The home valuation sends back a written read instead of an instant number, because the instant number is the part everybody distrusts. The video work is built for vertical rather than cropped into it.
  - The identity came first. A JK monogram with icon and wordmark variants, a palette specified for print as well as screen, and typefaces assigned by role so the system still holds when somebody else picks it up. Compliance was designed into the build rather than added at the end.
  - The hard decision was the valuation. Every competitor hands back a number in four seconds, and a number in four seconds is worthless to a seller and a liability to an agent. Hers takes a day and comes back written, with the comparables it leaned on. It converts worse on paper and better in practice, because the people willing to wait are the people actually selling.
story:
  caps:
    - <b>What she had.</b> A name, a tagline, and a list of the places you could follow her. It worked as a link in bio and nothing beyond that, so a stranger landing on it had no way to decide anything.
    - <b>Set out before drawn.</b> Cap height, baseline and a centre axis, with the mark boxed to the width it has to hold at. A monogram that is going on a sign and a profile picture gets measured first.
    - <b>Then traced.</b> The J is one sweep. The K crosses it, and the counter left between them is pitched like a roof, with the dot sitting under it as a door. It reads as initials first and a house second, which is the right way round.
    - <b>Filled, and named.</b> The outlines close into solid and the wordmark sets underneath at the measure the set-out fixed. Icon, monogram and full lockup all come out of the same drawing.
    - <b>Then the variants.</b> Icon, wordmark and full lockup, every one cut out of the same drawing rather than redrawn to fit. The icon is what survives a profile picture; the lockup is what goes on a sign outside a house.
    - <b>Then the system.</b> Colour specified for print as well as screen, and typefaces assigned by role, so it still holds when somebody else picks it up.
    - <b>Then the site.</b> Built on the finished identity rather than around a theme. Listings feed in live, the valuation sends back a written read instead of an instant number, and compliance was designed in rather than added at the end.
