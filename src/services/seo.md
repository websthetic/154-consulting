explainer:
  heading: Search runs in layers, and most businesses skip the cheapest one.
  paragraphs:
    - Technical is whether search can read you at all. Pages have to be crawlable, fast enough, indexed rather than accidentally blocked, and marked up so a crawler knows a price is a price and an address is an address. It is unglamorous and it is usually where the first month of gains is sitting untouched.
    - On-page is whether each page is unmistakably about one thing. One term per page, carried in the title tag, the heading and the writing, with internal links that tell search which pages matter. A page trying to rank for six things reliably ranks for none of them.
    - Local is the layer most service businesses skip and the one with the shortest payback. A Google Business Profile that matches the site line for line, consistent name address and phone wherever you are listed, a real page per location, and reviews asked for properly. If your customers come from a catchment area rather than the whole country, start here.
  glossary:
    - term: Crawling
      definition: Search reading your pages. If it cannot crawl a page, nothing else about that page matters.
    - term: Indexing
      definition: Being stored in the search database. Crawled and indexed are two different things.
    - term: Title tag
      definition: The clickable line in the results. The single highest leverage piece of text on any page.
    - term: Meta description
      definition: The grey text under the title. It does not rank you, but it decides who clicks.
    - term: Schema markup
      definition: Code that tells search what your content is, which is how ratings and hours appear.
    - term: Map pack
      definition: The three local listings above the normal results. Driven by your Business Profile, not your site.

faq:
  - q: How long before SEO works?
    a: Technical fixes and the Business Profile can move local results inside a month. Competitive terms take longer, usually three to six months of content and links. Anyone promising page one in two weeks is selling you something else.
  - q: Do I need SEO if I get referrals?
    a: Referrals have a ceiling and they go quiet without warning. Search adds the people who have never heard your name, which is the part referrals cannot do.
  - q: Is local SEO different from regular SEO?
    a: Yes. Local turns on your Google Business Profile, consistent listings, reviews and pages built per location. For a service business with a catchment area it is usually the fastest return available.
