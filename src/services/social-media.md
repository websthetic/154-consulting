explainer:
  heading: A plan is knowing what each post is for.
  paragraphs:
    - "Every post should be doing one of three jobs: helping a stranger find you, giving somebody who already found you a reason to trust you, or asking for the work. A feed where everything is doing the third job reads as a billboard. A feed where nothing is reads as a hobby."
    - Cadence beats volume, every time. Three posts a week for a year will do more for a service business than twenty posts in a good month followed by silence through a busy one. The calendar exists so the quiet months do not happen.
    - And it has to look like you. Templates drawn from your own brand kit, captions in your register rather than a marketing voice, photography from your actual business. The reason most small business social reads as generic is that it was made from a template that was never yours.
  glossary:
    - term: Content pillar
      definition: A recurring theme you post about, so the feed has a shape instead of being random.
    - term: Cadence
      definition: How often you post. Consistency matters more than frequency.
    - term: Reach
      definition: How many separate people saw it. Not the same as impressions, which counts repeats.
    - term: Engagement rate
      definition: Interactions as a share of reach. Tells you whether the content landed or just appeared.
    - term: Saves and shares
      definition: The signals worth watching. Both mean somebody thought it was useful enough to keep or pass on.
    - term: Community management
      definition: Replying to comments and messages. For a service business this is usually where the work comes from.

faq:
  - q: Which platforms do you cover?
    a: Instagram and Facebook as standard, LinkedIn where the buyer is there, TikTok when there is appetite for video. We would rather do two properly than six badly.
  - q: Do I have to be in the content?
    a: No, though founder-led content usually outperforms everything else for a service business. If you would rather stay off camera we build around the work instead.
  - q: Can you post, or only design?
    a: Both. Most clients take the full service, design and scheduling and replies. Some take the templates and run it themselves, which is fine too.
