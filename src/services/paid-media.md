explainer:
  heading: Paid search and paid social are not the same job.
  paragraphs:
    - Somebody typing physiotherapist near me has already decided. The ad just has to be the most relevant answer, which means the copy names the thing, the place and the next step. Somebody scrolling a feed has decided nothing, so the creative has to earn attention before it can ask for anything. Running the same words in both places is the most common reason paid spend disappears.
    - You are not buying clicks, you are buying an auction position, and relevance changes what that position costs. Tighter targeting, better creative and a page that matches the promise all lower the price of the same click. That is why the landing page is a paid media decision rather than a web one.
    - None of it means anything without conversion tracking. If you cannot see which click became an enquiry and which enquiry became a customer, you are optimising towards traffic, and traffic is not the thing you wanted.
  glossary:
    - term: CPC
      definition: Cost per click. What you pay each time somebody clicks, set by auction rather than by you.
    - term: CPM
      definition: Cost per thousand impressions. How feed advertising is usually priced.
    - term: Quality Score
      definition: Google's view of how relevant your ad and page are. Higher relevance means a lower cost per click.
    - term: Conversion tracking
      definition: The wiring that tells you which click turned into an enquiry. Without it you are guessing.
    - term: Retargeting
      definition: Showing ads to people who already visited. Cheap, and easy to overdo.
    - term: Negative keywords
      definition: Terms you tell Google to ignore. Usually the fastest way to stop wasting budget.
