The questionnaire

Filled in before anything gets made. It's the source of truth for the whole project, and the line the work should hang on is usually buried in one of the last answers, so we read it to the end.

  • BrandWhat you sell, who it's for, and what you want people to feel.
  • WebWhat the site has to do, and who is actually landing on it.
  • GrowthWhere the work comes from today, and where you want it from.

Decisions, written down

Every judgement call gets made and recorded before anything is designed, built or spent. This is the step most studios skip, and it's the reason their projects drift.

  • BrandThe one sentence, the tone, and what the mark has to survive.
  • WebWhich page does the heavy lifting, the single action, and the order that earns it.
  • GrowthWhich channel goes first, which number we're moving, and what we stop doing.

Picking the mark

You see real directions, not one safe option with two throwaways beside it to make it look good. We narrow it down with you, out loud, and you hear why each one is in or out.

  • WebTested in the nav and at favicon size before anyone signs it off.

Questions
worth asking.

The ones that come up on nearly every first call, answered before you have to make the call.

What does a brand and website project cost?

It depends on scope, and I'd rather give you an honest number than post one that fits nobody. A brand system on its own is a different job from a ten page site with a live listings feed and a CRM behind it. Book twenty minutes and you'll leave with a real range.

How long does it take?

No six month timelines. A brand system usually runs a few weeks. A full site depends on how fast the questionnaire comes back and how quickly you can get to the reviews, because the process runs in order. Those two things are what slow projects down, not the build.

Do you only work with one type of business?

No. We've worked across real estate, education, wellness, food and drink, automotive and professional services. What clients have in common isn't an industry, it's a stage. They've outgrown word of mouth and there's nothing structured underneath yet.

Where do you work?

Across Ontario. Current clients are in the Greater Toronto Area, Durham Region, Mississauga and Markham. Everything runs remotely with scheduled reviews, so location has never been the constraint.

Can I edit the site myself after launch?

Yes. Every build is handed over with a content management system so you can change text, images and listings without coming back to me. Ongoing support is there if you want it, but it's an option, not a condition of launching.

Do you do brand only, or website only?

Either. Plenty of projects are just a brand system, or a site built on an identity you already own. The one thing I won't do is build a site on a foundation that doesn't exist yet, because that's how you end up paying for it twice.

Who actually does the work?

I lead every project: the questionnaire, the structure, the flow and the design direction. My designer handles identity and print. My developer builds from the spec I write. One team, and you're dealing with me the whole way through.

What isn't included?

Trademark searches and legal registration sit outside scope on every project. Photography, video and ad spend are quoted separately. Every proposal says so in writing, so nothing gets assumed either way.

What happens after the site goes live?

Growth. Search first, since it works on what you already own, then content and paid depending on where the pipeline is thinnest. The site is the asset. Growth is what puts people in front of it.
