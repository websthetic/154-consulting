--- title: Brand and website questions answered | 154 Consulting description: What a brand and website project costs, how long it takes, who actually does the work, and exactly what you own at the end of it. permalink: /faq/ schemaType: faq faq: - q: What does a brand and website project cost? a: It depends on scope, and I'd rather give you an honest number than post one that fits nobody. A brand system on its own is a different job from a ten page site with a live listings feed and a CRM behind it. Book twenty minutes and you'll leave with a real range. - q: How long does it take? a: No six month timelines. A brand system usually runs a few weeks. A full site depends on how fast the questionnaire comes back and how quickly you can get to the reviews, because the process runs in order. Those two things are what slow projects down, not the build. - q: Do you only work with one type of business? a: No. We've worked across real estate, education, wellness, food and drink, automotive and professional services. What clients have in common isn't an industry, it's a stage. They've outgrown word of mouth and there's nothing structured underneath yet. - q: Where do you work? a: Across Ontario. Current clients are in the Greater Toronto Area, Durham Region, Mississauga and Markham. Everything runs remotely with scheduled reviews, so location has never been the constraint. - q: Can I edit the site myself after launch? a: Yes. Every build is handed over with a content management system so you can change text, images and listings without coming back to me. Ongoing support is there if you want it, but it's an option, not a condition of launching. - q: Do you do brand only, or website only? a: Either. Plenty of projects are just a brand system, or a site built on an identity you already own. The one thing I won't do is build a site on a foundation that doesn't exist yet, because that's how you end up paying for it twice. - q: Who actually does the work? a: "I lead every project: the questionnaire, the structure, the flow and the design direction. My designer handles identity and print. My developer builds from the spec I write. One team, and you're dealing with me the whole way through." - q: What isn't included? a: Trademark searches and legal registration sit outside scope on every project. Photography, video and ad spend are quoted separately. Every proposal says so in writing, so nothing gets assumed either way. - q: What happens after the site goes live? a: Growth. Search first, since it works on what you already own, then content and paid depending on where the pipeline is thinnest. The site is the asset. Growth is what puts people in front of it. ---
